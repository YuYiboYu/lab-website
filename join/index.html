Join Us

We are looking for self-motivated students who are excited to build practical networked systems for immersive computing, extended reality, human-AI interaction, volumetric media, and mobile/edge/cloud platforms.

Ph.D. Students

We welcome Ph.D. applicants with strong hands-on experience and interest in building real systems. Relevant backgrounds include:

  • Computer networks
  • Mobile computing
  • Distributed systems
  • Edge and cloud computing
  • Extended reality
  • Computer vision
  • Graphics
  • Machine learning
  • Human-computer interaction
  • Web systems
  • Security and privacy

Applicants who enjoy building prototypes, running experiments, analyzing system performance, and publishing in top research venues are especially encouraged to apply.

M.S. Students

M.S. students can contribute to active research projects through system development, experimentation, data analysis, benchmarking, visualization, and software engineering.

Strong candidates usually have experience with at least one of the following:

  • Python, JavaScript, C++, or TypeScript
  • Web systems or WebXR
  • Unity, Unreal, or 3D visualization tools
  • Machine learning or computer vision
  • Networking or distributed systems
  • Data analysis and visualization

Undergraduate Researchers

Undergraduate students can join projects involving software development, prototype building, experiment design, visualization, and data collection.

Students interested in research are encouraged to prepare:

  • A short statement of interest
  • Relevant coursework
  • Prior projects
  • GitHub or portfolio link, if available
  • Weekly time availability

Current Research Opportunities

We are especially interested in students who want to work on:

  • Multi-user extended reality
  • Immersive content delivery
  • Volumetric video streaming
  • Collaborative immersive analytics
  • Human-AI interaction
  • Browser-based XR and WebGPU systems
  • Privacy-preserving spatial computing
  • Mobile, edge, and cloud systems for immersive applications
