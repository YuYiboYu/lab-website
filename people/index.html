Collaborators

The lab collaborates with researchers from George Mason University, national laboratories, industry research labs, and other universities on immersive computing, mobile systems, extended reality, spatial computing, and networked platforms.

Representative collaboration areas include volumetric video streaming, mobile 3D vision, privacy-preserving spatial computing, browser-based acceleration, and collaborative immersive analytics.
