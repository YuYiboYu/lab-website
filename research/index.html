Research

The [Lab Name] develops practical networked systems for emerging immersive and intelligent applications. Our research combines systems, networking, extended reality, mobile computing, edge/cloud infrastructure, computer vision, graphics, privacy, and human-AI interaction.

Theme 1: Immersive Content Delivery

Immersive applications require high-quality visual content under strict bandwidth, latency, and device constraints. We study how to deliver 360-degree video, volumetric video, 6DoF content, and spatial media efficiently to mobile and XR devices.

Representative topics include viewport-aware streaming, volumetric video delivery, neural content representation, semantic communication, remote rendering, and adaptive immersive media systems.

Related areas: volumetric video, 360-degree video, neural rendering, semantic communication, mobile streaming.

Theme 2: Multi-user Extended Reality

Future immersive applications will involve multiple users interacting in shared physical, virtual, and mixed environments. We design systems that support scalable, low-latency, and synchronized multi-user XR experiences.

Representative topics include collaborative AR/MR, social VR measurement, multi-user volumetric streaming, shared spatial anchors, and cross-device immersive collaboration.

Related areas: collaborative XR, social VR, multi-user AR, immersive telepresence, shared spatial computing.

Theme 3: Human-AI Interaction

Generative AI is becoming part of daily communication, collaboration, and decision-making. We study how humans interact with AI systems and how future networked platforms can support human-AI communication.

Representative topics include human-to-GenAI calling, AI-mediated collaboration, multimodal interaction, and measurement of AI-supported communication systems.

Related areas: generative AI, human-AI communication, interactive systems, multimodal collaboration.

Theme 4: Mobile, Edge, and Cloud Systems for XR

Immersive applications often exceed the compute, memory, energy, and networking capacity of standalone devices. We investigate how mobile devices, edge servers, and cloud systems can work together to support responsive immersive applications.

Representative topics include low-latency XR pipelines, edge-assisted AR, cloud rendering, browser-based acceleration, WebAssembly, WebGPU, and mobile immersive computing infrastructure.

Related areas: mobile computing, edge computing, cloud systems, WebXR, WebGPU, WebAssembly.

Theme 5: Privacy and Trust in Spatial Computing

XR devices continuously sense users, environments, gestures, gaze, images, spatial maps, and physical context. We design privacy-preserving methods that allow immersive applications to function while reducing exposure of sensitive user and environment data.

Representative topics include privacy-preserving 6DoF localization, spatial mapping, user authentication, federated learning, and secure immersive sensing.

Related areas: XR privacy, spatial data security, authentication, federated learning, privacy-preserving localization.

Theme 6: Measurement of Emerging Immersive Platforms

Emerging immersive platforms often make strong claims about performance, interaction, and user experience. We conduct measurement studies that reveal real-world limitations, opportunities, and system bottlenecks.

Representative topics include social VR platforms, Apple Vision Pro, web-based XR, online education in metaverse environments, browser acceleration, and immersive telepresence.

Related areas: measurement, performance analysis, platform evaluation, user experience, systems benchmarking.
