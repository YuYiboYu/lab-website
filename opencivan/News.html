News

OpenCIVAN Project Initiative Established

OpenCIVAN is being developed as an open-source ecosystem for collaborative immersive visualization and analytics. The initiative builds on ParaView's scientific visualization foundation and ongoing work on XR and CAVE interfaces.

Community Discovery and User Engagement

The project team is engaging researchers, developers, educators, government organizations, and industry partners to understand their visualization needs and identify opportunities for collaboration.

Workshops, Webinars, and Hackathons

Future community activities will include workshops, webinars, tutorials, and hackathons focused on immersive visualization, collaborative analysis, networked computing, and scientific applications.

Contribute to the Roadmap

Community feedback will help shape OpenCIVAN's technical priorities, documentation, onboarding resources, and future integrations.
