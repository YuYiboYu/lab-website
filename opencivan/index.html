OpenCIVAN: An Open-Source Ecosystem for Collaborative Immersive Visualization and Analytics

Open Collaborative Immersive Visualization and Analytics Network
Collaborative science, experienced in three dimensions.

Scientific data is becoming larger, more multidimensional, and more difficult to understand through conventional displays alone. OpenCIVAN brings researchers, educators, engineers, and other domain experts into a shared immersive environment where they can explore data, discuss findings, and analyze complex scientific phenomena together.

Built on the ParaView open-source visualization platform, OpenCIVAN connects immersive interfaces, collaborative interaction, networked computing, and high-performance computing resources in one extensible ecosystem.

What OpenCIVAN Enables

1

Explore scientific datasets through virtual, augmented, and mixed reality interfaces.

2

Collaborate in real time across geographically distributed locations.

3

Use immersive displays, head-mounted devices, and multi-wall CAVE environments.

4

Connect interactive visualization with cloud and high-performance computing resources.

5

Develop and share new visualization, analytics, and collaboration capabilities through an open-source community.

Why It Matters

Many scientific datasets contain spatial, temporal, and relational structures that are difficult to inspect on a conventional two-dimensional screen. OpenCIVAN is intended to make these structures more accessible by supporting interactive, immersive, and shared analysis.

The ecosystem is designed for applications in scientific research, education, healthcare, climate science, manufacturing, engineering, urban planning, public health, and other areas where teams must understand complex data and make informed decisions.

Platform Overview

OpenCIVAN builds on ParaView's data processing and visualization capabilities and extends them toward collaborative immersive analytics. Its planned ecosystem brings together:

1

ParaView for data processing, analysis, and geometry generation.

2

XR interfaces for immersive interaction through supported devices.

3

CAVE interfaces for room-scale and multi-wall visualization.

4

WebXR, WebAssembly, and WebGPU for flexible networked and cross-platform deployment.

5

Cloud and HPC resources for handling large and computationally demanding datasets.

OpenCIVAN is being developed as a community-oriented ecosystem. Researchers, developers, educators, institutions, and technology partners are invited to help shape its future.
