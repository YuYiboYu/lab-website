Working Groups

OpenCIVAN depends on several connected areas of work. The working groups below provide a clear way for contributors to participate while keeping the technical and community goals aligned.

1

Immersive Interfaces

This group develops and improves interaction through virtual, augmented, and mixed reality. Activities may include support for head-mounted displays, OpenXR integration, spatial interaction, tracking, multi-user presence, and CAVE environments.

Example contributions: device integration, interaction techniques, visualization controls, accessibility, and immersive user studies.

2

Collaborative Systems and Networking

This group focuses on shared state, synchronization, communication, latency, multi-site interaction, and reliable data exchange among collaborators and visualization endpoints.

Example contributions: synchronization services, session management, network adaptation, remote collaboration, and privacy-aware communication.

3

Visualization and Analytics

This group develops methods for exploring scientific data in immersive and collaborative settings. It connects ParaView's data-processing capabilities with interaction techniques that help users inspect patterns, anomalies, structures, and relationships.

Example contributions: scientific workflows, multi-dimensional visual encodings, linked views, spatial analysis, and domain-specific applications.

4

Web and Platform Engineering

This group supports cross-platform access and scalable deployment through WebXR, WebAssembly, WebGPU, cloud infrastructure, and related technologies.

Example contributions: browser-based deployment, performance optimization, GPU acceleration, service interfaces, and platform interoperability.

5

HPC and Data Infrastructure

This group addresses the movement, processing, and visualization of large scientific datasets. It works across data sources, geometry servers, remote computation, storage, and interactive analysis pipelines.

Example contributions: remote visualization, HPC integration, streaming, data reduction, scalable processing, and reproducible workflows.

6

Community, Education, and Outreach

This group helps users and contributors learn, participate, and remain engaged. It supports documentation, tutorials, webinars, workshops, hackathons, forums, mentorship, and recognition of contributions.

Example contributions: onboarding materials, example applications, training sessions, community events, and contributor support.

7

Evaluation and Sustainability

This group evaluates technical progress, usability, adoption, community health, and long-term sustainability.

Example contributions: user studies, surveys, interviews, metrics dashboards, release evaluation, impact assessment, and sustainability planning.

Join a Working Group

You do not need to be an expert in every part of the ecosystem. Contributors can participate through software development, visualization research, infrastructure work, documentation, user feedback, education, or domain expertise.
