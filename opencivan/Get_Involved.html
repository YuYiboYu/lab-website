Get Involved

OpenCIVAN is intended to grow through contributions from people with different backgrounds and forms of expertise. You can participate by writing code, testing prototypes, proposing use cases, improving documentation, organizing events, or sharing feedback from a scientific or educational domain.

Ways to Contribute

Use and Test the Software

Try OpenCIVAN components with your datasets and workflows. Report bugs, usability barriers, performance problems, and missing capabilities through the project issue tracker.

Contribute Code

Review open issues, propose a feature, submit a merge request, or help improve integration with ParaView, XR devices, CAVE systems, WebXR, WebAssembly, WebGPU, and HPC environments.

Share a Use Case

Tell us how immersive and collaborative analytics could support your research, teaching, engineering, or public-interest work. Domain-specific use cases will help guide the ecosystem's development.

Improve Documentation and Education

Help create tutorials, examples, installation guides, troubleshooting pages, and training materials for new users and developers.

Participate in the Community

Join workshops, webinars, hackathons, user interviews, and feedback sessions. Community participation is valuable even when it does not involve writing software.

New Contributor Path

  1. 1

    Read the project overview and contribution guidelines.

  2. 2

    Join the community communication channel or forum.

  3. 3

    Select a working group that matches your interests.

  4. 4

    Start with an introductory issue, documentation improvement, or testing task.

  5. 5

    Discuss larger changes with maintainers before implementation.

  6. 6

    Submit your contribution for review and help improve it through community feedback.

Community Expectations

OpenCIVAN is committed to respectful, constructive, and inclusive collaboration. All contributors should follow the project's code of conduct, respect different levels of experience, document their work, and communicate openly about technical decisions.

Whether you are a researcher with a challenging dataset, a developer interested in XR systems, an educator exploring immersive learning, or an institution seeking collaborative visualization tools, we invite you to connect with OpenCIVAN.
